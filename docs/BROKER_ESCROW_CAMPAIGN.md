# Broker escrow and referral campaign

Created October 1, 2026. Audience: U.S. business and franchise brokers with relevant international-buyer clients. Goal: a qualified reply requesting written terms or discussing a client. Ten automatic emails only; no LinkedIn, calls, SMS, open triggers or click triggers.

## Positioning and claim basis

Jamie confirmed that everything is refunded if E-2 approval is not granted. Copy states that funds are held in escrow pending approval and refunded in full if approval is not granted. It does not label business performance or visa approval risk-free. Operating funding, management and obligations before approval remain a discussion topic rather than an invented promise. No invented visa-approval count, success rate, commission percentage, return or investment amount appears.

Commission eligibility, registration before first substantive contact, payment within 30 days after New Dawn receives escrow funds, and proportional installment payments follow the current broker agreement. The exact commission rate remains in the separate written schedule. Full-refund marketing should be reflected consistently in the escrow agreement provided to each client.

## Research and choices

- [Gong cold-email research](https://www.gong.io/blog/does-cold-email-even-work-any-more-heres-what-the-data-says): its observational dataset supports short, clear messages under 100 words. Here each body is 55–74 words, excluding the sender signature and unsubscribe footer. We use one reply request and a concrete reason for each touch.
- [Mailchimp subject guidance](https://mailchimp.com/help/best-practices-for-email-subject-lines/): favor descriptive, concise subjects and audience testing. These are 17–24 characters, with one alternate per email for a later controlled test. No fake reply prefixes, urgency or approval guarantees.
- [Mailchimp preview guidance](https://mailchimp.com/help/about-preview-text/): use a short complementary detail, with the useful information first. Each editable preview is 67–74 characters. Actual inbox display and truncation vary by client.
- [Belkins follow-up analysis](https://belkins.io/blog/sales-follow-up-statistics): its managed-campaign dataset finds diminishing returns from longer sequences, with a practical sweet spot around three to five steps. Ten is the requested nurture design, not a research-proven optimum. The first five span three weeks; the other five are progressively spaced out to day 90.
- [Belkins send-time analysis](https://belkins.io/blog/best-time-to-send-email): morning sends are a reasonable test baseline; the data does not establish a universal best time or perfectly resolve recipient timezones. This pilot uses weekdays 9 AM–noon **America/Chicago**, not inferred recipient-local time. Once recipient geography is known, compare regional windows using qualified replies and meetings.
- [Mailchimp send-time optimization](https://mailchimp.com/help/use-send-time-optimization/): audience engagement history is more useful than treating a generic time as definitive. New Dawn does not yet have reliable campaign evidence establishing a winning window.
- [State Department E-2 guidance, 9 FAM 402.9](https://fam.state.gov/fam/09FAM/09FAM040209.html): visa-contingent escrow can coexist with the E-2 requirement that investment be at commercial risk. This is why copy describes the refund condition directly rather than claiming an entirely risk-free investment.

## Cadence and controls

Days are minimum offsets from enrollment; actual follow-up spacing is preserved from the previous actual send. Weekends, pauses, review holds and caps can extend the sequence. The ten-email policy allows up to ten emails per recipient across campaigns in 120 days, and stops enrollment backlog after 120 days. Other campaigns retain the existing three-email / 45-day policy.

The global delivery pause, 80/day and 15/hour settings, suppression checks, current valid email verification, seven-day same-firm separation and 24-hour recipient cooldown remain. Recipient fit must be re-reviewed at least every 30 days; this campaign will hold when review expires. No quiet automatic bypass occurs for later touches. Replies stop active sequences; opt-outs suppress further outreach. Existing relationship and meeting handling remains in place. Clicks and opens do not schedule a call or trigger another message. The final email completes the sequence with no automatic recycling.

Replies go to Dylan's sending mailbox, dylan@newdawnfranchising.com, and the existing mailbox sync records matched campaign replies. The email service adds Dylan's minimal signature, a physical mailing address and recipient-specific unsubscribe links/headers. The campaign body adds no tracking pixels or tracked links. Internal qualification notes are not prepended to this broker nurture copy.

No recipients are enrolled. Campaign is saved paused because the earlier external delivery test landed in Spam despite passing authentication. Creating this campaign does not clear that delivery issue or authorize a nationwide blast.

## Measurement and testing

Primary measure: qualified replies divided by accepted recipients, alongside booked meetings and eligible referral introductions. Also report opt-outs, verified bounces, complaints if available, and holds by reason. Accepted by SMTP is not proof of inbox placement. Opens/clicks are not qualification metrics.

Use the primary subjects first. On a sufficiently sized qualified pilot, randomize comparable recipients between the primary and alternate subject for the first email, keeping the body and timing fixed. Choose on qualified replies after allowing the same follow-up period; do not claim a winner from a handful of responses. Later test regional timing separately. There are no fabricated response-rate targets or revenue forecasts. Review results after the initial five emails before expanding the audience; do not increase volume while inbox placement remains unresolved.
