Upgrade the Prospect Finder in the New Dawn Franchising CRM to move beyond basic 
business listings (name, address, phone) and instead surface individual decision-makers 
with verified contact details and a seniority score. Below is the full multi-phase 
methodology to implement.

The current Prospect Finder searches by 7 categories (immigration attorneys, E-2 visa 
consulting firms, business brokers, immigration consultants, franchise consultants, 
CPAs international tax, relocation services) filtered by US state or country. The 
problem is it returns companies — we need PEOPLE, specifically the partners, owners, 
and managing attorneys who make referral decisions.

---

PHASE 1 — SEED DATA (What You Already Have)
Keep the existing search layer (Google Places API or similar) to discover businesses 
by category and location. This returns: business name, address, main phone, website 
URL, and Google rating. This is the outer shell. Do not show these results to the user 
yet — feed them into Phase 2 automatically.

---

PHASE 2 — WEBSITE INTELLIGENCE SCRAPING
For every business returned in Phase 1, scrape their website to find individuals. 
Use Playwright (not basic fetch) because many firm websites are JavaScript-rendered.

Target these URL patterns on each domain:
/team, /about, /attorneys, /staff, /partners, /our-team, /people, /professionals, 
/meet-the-team, /leadership, /lawyers, /consultants

On each of those pages, extract using NLP + regex:
- Full names (pattern: capitalized first + last name near a title keyword)
- Job titles (look for: Owner, Founder, Partner, Principal, Managing Attorney, 
  Senior Attorney, Director, Associate, Consultant, Advisor)
- Direct email addresses (regex: any email on the page, especially name@domain patterns)
- Direct phone extensions or personal phone numbers
- LinkedIn profile URLs (look for linkedin.com/in/ links)
- Headshot image alt text (often contains the person's name and title)
- Bio snippets mentioning E-2 visa, international clients, immigration, franchising

If no team page is found, fall back to scraping the homepage and contact page 
for any individual names mentioned.

---

PHASE 3 — PROFESSIONAL DIRECTORY MATCHING
After website scraping, cross-reference against authoritative professional 
directories for each category. These directories list verified individual members 
with their specializations. Implement scrapers or API integrations for:

IMMIGRATION ATTORNEYS:
- AILA.org (American Immigration Lawyers Association) — member directory is 
  searchable by city/state. Extracts: attorney name, firm, bar admission, 
  specializations, direct contact. Cross-reference AILA membership as a 
  quality signal (AILA members = verified immigration specialists).
- State Bar Association websites — every US state has a publicly searchable 
  attorney directory at their bar association site (e.g., texasbar.com, 
  calbar.ca.gov). Search by city + practice area "immigration." Returns 
  licensed attorney names, bar numbers, firm affiliation, office address.
- Avvo.com — has detailed attorney profiles with specialization ratings, 
  contact info, and client reviews. Scrape name, title, specializations, 
  and whether they list "E-2 visa" as a practice area.

BUSINESS BROKERS (INTERNATIONAL):
- IBBA.org (International Business Brokers Association) — member directory 
  with individual broker names, certifications (CBI = Certified Business 
  Intermediary, a quality signal), and contact details.
- BizBuySell.com broker listings — scrape broker profile pages for name, 
  bio mentioning international deals, direct contact.

FRANCHISE CONSULTANTS:
- FranServe.com and FranChoice.com — both have consultant directories 
  with individual profiles, territories covered, and contact info.
- IFA.org (International Franchise Association) member directory.

CPAs (INTERNATIONAL TAX):
- State CPA Society websites (e.g., tscpa.org for Texas) — searchable 
  member directories with individual CPA names and firm affiliations.
- CPADirectory.com — searchable by location and specialty. Filter for 
  "international tax" or "foreign national."

IMMIGRATION CONSULTANTS & RELOCATION SERVICES:
- Worldwide ERC (worldwideerc.org) — relocation industry association 
  directory with individual member profiles.
- LinkedIn search API / scraping as primary source for these categories 
  since formal directories are limited.

---

PHASE 4 — LINKEDIN ENRICHMENT (Decision Maker Discovery)
For each business found, use the Proxycurl API (proxycurl.com — they have 
a clean REST API specifically for LinkedIn data without violating ToS) to:

1. Find all employees at the company on LinkedIn
2. Filter by seniority keywords in title: Owner, Founder, Co-Founder, 
   Partner, Principal, Managing, Director, Senior, Head of, VP
3. For each decision-maker found, extract:
   - Full name
   - Exact job title
   - Years at current company
   - LinkedIn profile URL
   - Previous companies (indicates experience level)
   - Location (city/country)
   - Whether they have any posts mentioning E-2 visa, immigration, 
     franchise, or international investment in the last 90 days 
     (high-relevance signal)

Proxycurl API endpoints to use:
- /proxycurl/api/linkedin/company/employees/ (find all employees by company URL)
- /proxycurl/api/v2/linkedin (enrich individual profile by LinkedIn URL)

---

PHASE 5 — CONTACT DATA ENRICHMENT
Once you have a person's full name and company domain, run them through 
the following enrichment APIs (implement in waterfall order — stop when 
you have verified email + phone):

STEP A: Apollo.io API
Endpoint: POST /api/v1/people/match
Input: first_name, last_name, organization_domain
Returns: email address, direct phone, LinkedIn URL, job title, seniority 
level (C-suite / VP / Director / Manager / Individual), department.
Apollo has 275M+ contact records and is the best first stop.

STEP B: Hunter.io API (if Apollo returns no email)
Endpoint: GET /v2/email-finder?domain={domain}&first_name={}&last_name={}
Returns: most likely email address based on company's email pattern + 
a confidence score (0-100). Only use results with confidence > 70.
Also call: GET /v2/domain-search?domain={domain} to get the company's 
email pattern (e.g., {first}.{last}@domain.com) so you can construct 
emails for contacts Apollo missed.

STEP C: RocketReach API (if no mobile number yet)
Input: name + company
Returns: direct mobile numbers, which are harder to find than emails. 
RocketReach specializes in mobile numbers for professionals.

STEP D: Clearbit Enrichment API (company-level fallback)
If individual enrichment fails, use Clearbit to enrich at the company 
level and return the company's key contacts and tech stack.

---

PHASE 6 — VERIFICATION LAYER
Never store unverified contact data. Run every piece of contact info 
through verification before saving:

EMAIL VERIFICATION:
Use ZeroBounce API: POST /v2/validate
- "valid" status = safe to email
- "catch-all" status = store but flag as unconfirmed  
- "invalid" / "spamtrap" / "abuse" = discard entirely
Store a verified_email boolean and verification_date per contact.

PHONE VERIFICATION:
Use Twilio Lookup API: GET /v1/PhoneNumbers/{number}?Type=carrier
- Returns: line type (mobile vs. landline vs. VOIP)
- Mobile numbers = store as primary SMS/call target
- Landline = store as office number only (no SMS)
- Add country code normalization for international numbers
Store phone_type ("mobile" / "landline" / "voip") per number.

---

PHASE 7 — DECISION MAKER SCORING ALGORITHM
After all enrichment is complete, score every individual contact 
from 0–100 and display this as a "Decision Maker Score" badge in 
the UI. This tells the user who to reach out to first.

TITLE/SENIORITY SCORE (max 40 points):
- Owner / Founder / Co-Founder / Principal: 40 points
- Partner / Managing Partner / Managing Attorney: 35 points
- Director / Senior Director / VP: 28 points
- Senior Attorney / Senior Consultant / Senior Advisor: 22 points
- Associate Attorney / Consultant / Advisor: 12 points
- Paralegal / Assistant / Coordinator / Staff: 4 points

REACHABILITY SCORE (max 35 points):
- Has verified direct email (confidence > 85%): 15 points
- Has verified mobile number: 12 points
- Has LinkedIn profile URL: 5 points
- Has direct office number: 3 points

RELEVANCE SCORE (max 25 points):
- Bio or LinkedIn mentions "E-2 visa" specifically: 15 points
- Bio mentions "international clients" or "foreign national": 8 points
- LinkedIn activity in last 30 days (active = reachable): 5 points
- Company has 4+ star Google rating: 3 points
- AILA member or professional certification held: 4 points (can exceed 25 max — cap at 25)

Display score as a colored badge:
- 75–100: 🟢 Hot Lead (decision maker, verified contact, E-2 relevant)
- 50–74: 🟡 Warm Lead (decision maker or verified, partially relevant)
- 25–49: 🟠 Cold Lead (found but limited data)
- 0–24: ⚪ Unqualified (admin staff or no contact info)

---

PHASE 8 — DATA FRESHNESS & STALENESS MANAGEMENT
Contact data degrades over time. Implement:

- Store a last_verified_date timestamp for every email and phone number
- Auto-flag any contact where last_verified_date > 90 days old with 
  a "stale" indicator in the UI
- When a user tries to add a stale contact to a campaign, prompt: 
  "This contact was last verified 120 days ago. Re-verify before sending?"
- Track email bounce events: if a campaign email hard-bounces, 
  automatically mark that email as invalid and trigger re-enrichment
- Track SMS delivery failures: if an SMS fails to deliver, mark 
  the number as invalid

---

CACHING & RATE LIMIT MANAGEMENT
These APIs have rate limits and costs. Implement smart caching:
- Cache enriched company results for 30 days (don't re-query 
  Apollo/Proxycurl for the same domain within 30 days)
- Store all enriched data in the database, not just in memory
- Use a job queue (like Bull or similar) for enrichment tasks 
  so the UI doesn't hang — show a "Enriching data..." progress 
  bar while the pipeline runs in the background
- Prioritize enrichment of contacts the user explicitly saves 
  or hovers over (lazy enrichment for the rest)
