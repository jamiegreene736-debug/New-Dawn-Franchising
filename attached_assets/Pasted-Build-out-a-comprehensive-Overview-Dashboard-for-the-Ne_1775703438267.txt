## PART 2 — OVERVIEW DASHBOARD (/marketing-portal/dashboard)

The Overview Dashboard is the first thing a franchisee sees when
they log in. It must answer the question "how is my business doing
right now?" in under 10 seconds. Every metric is real-time or
updated within the last 24 hours. No metric is filler.

The dashboard is divided into six visual zones stacked vertically:

  ZONE 1 — Daily Agent Status Bar (full width, top of page)
  ZONE 2 — Primary KPI Cards (top metrics row)
  ZONE 3 — Pipeline Health Row
  ZONE 4 — Outreach Performance Row
  ZONE 5 — Two-column section: Activity Feed + Upcoming Actions
  ZONE 6 — Mini Charts Row (trend sparklines)

---

### ZONE 1 — DAILY AGENT STATUS BAR (full width banner)

A slim status bar pinned just below the portal nav. Always visible.
Changes colour and content based on current agent state.

States and colours:
  PENDING (yellow): "⏳ Today's outreach brief is ready and waiting
  for your approval. [View & Approve Brief →]"

  APPROVED (blue, pulsing dot): "🔵 Agent is executing — X messages
  are being sent right now. [View Activity →]"

  EXECUTED (green): "✅ Done for today — X emails sent, X WhatsApp
  sent, X letters queued. [View Summary →]"

  HELD (grey): "⏸ Today's outreach was held. Nothing was sent.
  [Review in Outreach →]"

  NOT EXECUTED (red): "⚠️ Today's brief expired without approval.
  Messages have rolled over to tomorrow. [Review →]"

  NO BRIEF YET (neutral): "Agent is preparing today's brief.
  Check back at 7:00 AM."

The banner is always one click away from the relevant action.

### ZONE 2 — PRIMARY KPI CARDS (8 cards in a responsive grid)

Display these 8 metric cards in a 4x2 grid (desktop) or 2x4 (mobile).
Each card shows: metric name, current value (large), comparison delta
(vs last 7 days or last month), and a mini trend indicator (up/down arrow
with percentage change). Cards use colour-coded status: green = good,
amber = watch, red = needs attention.

CARD 1 — TOTAL ACTIVE LEADS
Value: Total number of leads currently in the pipeline
(excludes Cold tier and DNC)
Delta: "+X new this week"
Colour: neutral — informational
Click: goes to Pipeline page

CARD 2 — HOT LEADS 🔴
Value: Number of leads currently scoring 80+
Delta: "+X vs last week"
Colour: red accent — signals urgency
Click: goes to Pipeline filtered to HOT tier
Sub-line: "X have a verified address — letter ready"

CARD 3 — CONSULTATIONS BOOKED
Value: Total Zoom/call consultations booked this month
Delta: "+X vs last month"
Colour: green if above target, amber if below
Click: goes to bookings calendar
Sub-line: "Next call: [Date, Time] with [Lead First Name]"

CARD 4 — OUTREACH SENT TODAY
Value: Total messages sent today (emails + WhatsApp combined)
Delta: "X emails / X WhatsApp"
Colour: green if brief was approved, grey if not yet
Click: goes to Outreach sent log

CARD 5 — EMAIL OPEN RATE (last 30 days)
Value: Percentage displayed large (e.g. "42%")
Delta: "+X% vs last month"
Colour: green if above 35%, amber if 20-35%, red if below 20%
Click: goes to Reports — email performance

CARD 6 — REPLY RATE (last 30 days)
Value: Percentage of outreach emails that received a reply
Delta: "+X% vs last month"
Colour: green if above 8%, amber if 4-8%, red if below 4%
Click: goes to Reports — reply performance

CARD 7 — ACTIVE MANAGED PROPERTIES
Value: Number of homeowner properties currently under management
Delta: "+X added this month"
Colour: always green (positive metric)
Click: goes to a property portfolio view (future feature placeholder)
Sub-line: "X new signed this month"

CARD 8 — PIPELINE VALUE (estimated)
Value: Estimated monthly recurring revenue if all WARM and HOT
leads convert (based on average management fee × property count)
Delta: "+$X vs last week"
Colour: green
Formula shown on hover: "Based on X leads × avg $[fee]/mo"
Click: goe
