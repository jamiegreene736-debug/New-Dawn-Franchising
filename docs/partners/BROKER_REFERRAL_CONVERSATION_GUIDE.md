# Broker referral conversations

Internal partnership-team reference. Not served by the website or included in the public client bundle. Use for direct conversations and individually provided partner materials. This guide does not replace the executed agreement or applicable disclosure requirements.

## Discussing compensation with a prospective broker

The owner-approved illustrative maximum is 12.5% of a qualifying $250,000 franchise sale, or $31,250. This is an example, not a typical-earnings claim, guaranteed payment, or a universal franchise investment price. Before quoting a client-specific amount, verify the applicable written agreement and commission schedule, including eligible payment basis, rate, conditions and timing. An introduction alone does not earn a fee. Do not apply this broker illustration to immigration attorneys.

Give the broker the current agreement and schedule in the direct conversation. Explain what work and conditions are involved; avoid describing it as effortless or guaranteed money. This website change does not amend existing agreements or change payment logic.

## Before introducing a client

- Explain that New Dawn may compensate the broker if the client completes a qualifying purchase. Explain the actual basis and relevant conditions, and provide the applicable disclosures early enough for an informed decision.
- Answer questions about compensation accurately. Moving recruitment terms out of public marketing does not justify withholding information from clients or avoiding required disclosures.
- Explain which party the broker represents and the actual scope of assistance. Do not imply that a paid sales relationship is an independent or fiduciary advisory relationship unless it is.
- Ask for permission before sharing client details; use only the information needed for the agreed introduction.
- Let the client evaluate alternatives and retain independent legal and financial advisers.

Conversation outline (adapt to verified facts):

“I have a referral relationship with New Dawn. If you complete a qualifying franchise purchase, New Dawn may pay my firm under our agreement. Let me explain how that compensation is calculated and the conditions that apply. You should evaluate whether the business meets your goals and review the investment with your own advisers.”

## Answering pricing questions

Before making any pricing comparison, verify the proposed package, written fees, commission arrangement and any separate broker charges. Do not claim “same price as going direct,” “no added cost,” or “the commission costs you nothing” without support for the precise claim. A lack of a separate broker invoice does not establish that compensation has no economic cost.

If asked, explain the client's actual written proposal and any referral-related charges. If a detail has not been verified, obtain it before answering. Provide relevant compensation information rather than directing the client away from the question.
