### WHO THE FRANCHISEE IS TARGETING

TARGET LEAD PROFILES:
- Homeowners with 1-10 rental properties in the territory
  who are currently self-managing (frustrated landlords)
- Homeowners who are relocating and need someone to manage
  their property while they are away
- Real estate investors who own properties in El Paso
  but live elsewhere
- New landlords who just bought an investment property
  and do not know where to start
- Homeowners who have had bad experiences with a previous
  property manager and are looking for a better option
- Real estate agents who regularly refer clients to
  property managers (referral partner leads)

LEAD DISCOVERY SOURCES:
- For-rent-by-owner listings on Zillow, Craigslist,
  Facebook Marketplace (self-managing homeowners = warm leads)
- Google Maps for landlords and property owners in the territory
- Facebook groups for El Paso landlords and real estate investors
- Nextdoor posts where homeowners ask about renting their property
- Public property records for multi-property owners in the territory
- Local real estate agent directories (referral partner targets)
- Neighbourhood forums and community boards
- Mortgage data for recently purchased investment properties
  in the territory (new landlords who will need management)

OUTREACH VOICE:
All outreach is written in the franchisee's own first-person voice.
The franchisee fills in their name, background, and writing samples
during onboarding and the AI adapts accordingly.

OUTREACH SEQUENCE FOR HOMEOWNERS (4-touch):
Touch 1 (Day 1): Soft intro referencing their specific listing
  or situation, one genuine question
Touch 2 (Day 4): Value — local market insight, landlord tip,
  or relevant resource
Touch 3 (Day 9): Direct ask — free 15-minute property management
  consultation via franchisee's Calendly link
Touch 4 (Day 16): Soft close — "happy to leave the door open"

PHYSICAL MAIL FOR HIGH-SCORING HOMEOWNER LEADS (Lob):
- Included as a recommendation in the franchisee's daily brief
- Never sent automatically — requires explicit franchisee approval
- Letter references the homeowner's specific property
- QR code links to franchisee's personal Calendly
- Threshold: lead score 80+ with verified property address

### FRANCHISEE LEAD SCORING (0-100)

CATEGORY 1 — INTENT SIGNALS (30 pts max)
  30 pts — Active for-rent-by-owner listing (self-managing now)
  25 pts — Posted in a landlord forum asking for advice
  20 pts — Commented on property management content
  15 pts — Posted about relocating and owning a property
  10 pts — General real estate investor activity
   5 pts — No signals but fits the profile

CATEGORY 2 — PROPERTY FIT (25 pts max)
  Sub-score A: Number of properties owned (10 pts)
    10 pts — 3+ properties (multi-property landlord)
     7 pts — 2 properties
     4 pts — 1 property confirmed
     1 pt  — Unknown
  Sub-score B: Property location (8 pts)
     8 pts — Property confirmed in NDF territory (El Paso)
     4 pts — Nearby area, possible territory fit
     0 pts — Outside territory
  Sub-score C: Property type (7 pts)
     7 pts — Single family home (core NDF property type)
     5 pts — Townhouse or condo
     3 pts — Multi-unit small building
     1 pt  — Unknown

CATEGORY 3 — LANDLORD PAIN SIGNALS (20 pts max)
  20 pts — Explicit complaints about self-managing
           (vacancy, difficult tenants, maintenance headaches)
  16 pts — Self-managing a property remotely
  12 pts — Self-managing for more than 1 year
   8 pts — Recently became a landlord (needs guidance)
   4 pts — Some landlord activity but limited signal
   0 pts — No pain signals detected

CATEGORY 4 — ENGAGEMENT & RECENCY (15 pts max)
  15 pts — Activity in the last 7 days on a relevant topic
  12 pts — Active in last 14 days
   9 pts — Last activity 15-30 days ago
   5 pts — Last activity 31-60 days ago
   2 pts — Last activity 61-90 days ago
   0 pts — No detectable activity in 90+ days

CATEGORY 5 — OUTREACH RESPONSIVENESS (10 pts max)
  10 pts — Replied to an email or WhatsApp
   8 pts — Opened an email 2+ times
   6 pts — Clicked a link in an email
   4 pts — Opened an email once
   2 pts — Email delivered, no open
   0 pts — No contact yet or email bounced
  -5 pts — Ignored across 3+ touches

TIER LABELS:
  80-100  🔴 HOT — Fast-track, prepare letter draft for brief
  60-79   🟠 WARM — Standard 4-touch sequence
  40-59   🟡 NURTURE — Monthly email only
  20-39   🔵 WATCH — Monitor only
  0-19    ⚪ COLD — No action

SCORE DECAY:
Engagement score decays 3pts per week after 30 days of inactivity.
Full re-score every Sunday at 5 AM.
Partial re-score (recency + responsiveness) nightly at 5:30 AM.
