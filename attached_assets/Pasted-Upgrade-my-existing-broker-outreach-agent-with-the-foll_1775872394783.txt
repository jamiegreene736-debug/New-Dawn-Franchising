CRM TIMELINE TAB — REQUIRED FEATURE:

When the agent initiates a sequence for any broker lead, it must immediately create a new tab inside that lead’s CRM card called “Outreach Timeline”. This tab must be queryable by name (e.g. “Look up Bonny Johnson” returns her card and timeline).

The Outreach Timeline tab must display every planned and completed action as a chronological list of events. Each event entry must include:

- The scheduled date and time for that action
- The channel (Email, SMS, LinkedIn, WhatsApp, Lob, HeyGen)
- The touch name (e.g. “Touch 1 — Soft Intro Email”, “Day 7 SMS Trigger”, “Lob Postcard”)
- The current status, shown with a clear visual indicator:
  - SCHEDULED — action is queued and pending
  - SENT — action was successfully delivered
  - OPENED — email or video was opened (where trackable)
  - CLICKED — link or CTA was clicked
  - REPLIED — broker responded
  - FAILED — action could not be completed (include reason, e.g. “Email bounced”, “Invalid phone number”, “HeyGen render error”)
  - SKIPPED — action was intentionally bypassed due to trigger conditions not being met (e.g. “SMS skipped — email not opened”)
  - PAUSED — sequence paused due to reply or meeting booked
  - CANCELLED — broker unsubscribed or marked Do Not Contact
- A notes field for any relevant detail (e.g. “Broker opened email 3 times”, “Lob postcard sent to 123 Main St”, “HeyGen video rendered successfully — link: [url]”)

The timeline must update in real time as each action executes. Dylan must be able to search any broker by name, open their CRM card, click the Outreach Timeline tab, and instantly see the full history and future schedule at a glance.
