Apollo.io

RocketReach


Query by company name and domain

ZoomInfo (Scrape Public Profiles)


Search site:zoominfo.com "[company name]" via Google
Scrape any publicly visible profile pages for name, title, email, phone


Clearbit


Use Clearbit's Enrichment API (person and company endpoints)
Great for enriching partial records (e.g., you have a name, get their email)


Lusha


Search public Lusha data for company employees
Pull direct dials and personal emails


Snov.io


Use the domain search endpoint to get all emails associated with a domain
Combine with name data from other sources


ContactOut


Search by LinkedIn URL or company name
Pulls personal emails and phone numbers LinkedIn hides


Crunchbase


Search the company on Crunchbase for founders, board members, executives
Pull funding rounds which often list key people by name and title


PitchBook (Google Scrape)


Run site:pitchbook.com "[company name]" queries
Extract any publicly visible people data


AngelList / Wellfound


Search for the company and pull all listed team members and job posters
Great for startups and franchise-style companies


Indeed & Glassdoor


Scrape job postings for the company — hiring manager names often appear

Facebook Business Pages


Scrape the company's Facebook page for tagged employees and admins

Better Business Bureau (BBB)


Search BBB for the business listing

Secretary of State / Business Registry (All 50 States)

Court Records & Litigation Databases

Google Maps / Yelp Business Listings
